# User guide

1. **Dashboard** shows rank, XP, accuracy, a skill radar and recent activity.
2. **Cases** → pick a case → choose a mode (Beginner shows analyst notes; Advanced earns more XP), optional timed challenge and hint penalties.
3. **Workspace** tabs: *Briefing* (story, entities, objectives), *Evidence* (open items to inspect, then **Collect** the ones you want to use), *Notes*, *Assistant* (explanations and graduated hints), *Verdict*.
4. **Evidence graph**: add collected items, connect them as supports / contradicts / precedes / related. On small screens or with a keyboard, use the list editor.
5. **Verdict**: choose a verdict, explain your reasoning, cite evidence, build the timeline (some events are unrelated), state alternatives and confidence, pick response actions (some are harmful), then submit.
6. **Report** explains every point: what you found, what you missed, misleading evidence you relied on, unsupported assumptions, and what the evidence cannot prove.
7. **Phishing Lab** and **Log Lab** are standalone practice with their own scoring. Links are never clickable.
8. **Profile**: achievements, settings, export/import backup, reset.

Tips: calibrate confidence to what the evidence can prove; hedged wording ("suggests", "not proven") is welcome. Replays only award XP for improving your best score.
